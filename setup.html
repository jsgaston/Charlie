Setup guide

Getting BearStack Pro running

Ten minutes, most of it waiting for MetaTrader to restart. If you only read one section, read the one about WebRequest — it is what stops nine out of ten first runs.

Install

Your purchase email carries two things: the file BearStackPro.ex5 and a licence key that looks like WSF-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. Keep the email; the key is not shown anywhere else.

  1. Put the file where MetaTrader looks

    In MetaTrader 5: File → Open Data Folder, then MQL5 → Experts. Drop BearStackPro.ex5 in there. Right-click the Navigator and choose Refresh, or restart the terminal.

  2. Allow the licence address

    Tools → Options → Expert Advisors. Tick Allow WebRequest for listed URL and add https://api.wisfli.com — exactly that, no trailing slash, no path. Click OK.

  3. Open an XAUUSD chart on M1

    The rules were built on gold on the one-minute chart. Other symbols and timeframes will run, but nothing about them has been tested and the EA will say so in the Journal.

  4. Drag the EA on and paste your key

    Drag BearStackPro from the Navigator onto the chart. In the Inputs tab, paste the key into Licence key. On the Common tab, tick Allow Algo Trading. Click OK, and make sure the Algo Trading button in the toolbar is green.

Check the Journal before you walk away

Open the Journal tab (not Experts) and look for two lines. One says Wisfli BSP | License active…. The other prints the magic number and the settings the EA started with. If the first line says anything else, the section on troubleshooting below tells you what it means.

A machine that stays on

BearStack Pro reads one-minute bars and manages a position on every tick. A laptop that sleeps, updates or loses wifi is not a platform for it.

VPS

Any Windows VPS near your broker's servers will do. MetaTrader's own hosting, from the terminal, is the least troublesome option if you do not want to manage a machine.

What a restart costs you

Nothing that is open: stops and targets live on the broker's side. What you lose is the management — break-even, trailing and the time exit stop happening until the terminal is back.

Your key travels with you

The same key works on three MetaTrader accounts, so moving from your desktop to a VPS needs no new purchase. Free an old account at api.wisfli.com/manage.

What the inputs do

The defaults are the ones running on the Armada account. You can change anything, but once you do, the published results no longer describe your setup.

Backtesting

The paid build runs in the Strategy Tester without a key, so you can test with the same file you trade with. There is also a free backtest build that runs in the tester only.

Settings that matter

Use Every tick based on real ticks if your broker provides them. On M1 with a 600-point stop, modelling from M1 open prices will flatter the result badly.

Set the spread to Current or to a realistic fixed value from your own account. Gold spreads widen at the open and around news, and this system trades in those windows.

What you cannot see in a test

Requotes, rejected orders and the real slippage on a fast move. Those are the difference between a demo record and a live one, and they work against a short-side system on gold more than most.

Test at least a full year, and look at the losing months rather than the total.

When something goes wrong

The EA prints its state in the Journal. Find the line that starts with Wisfli BSP and match it below.

Licence messages
Allow WebRequest for https://api.wisfli.comThe address is not on the list in Tools → Options → Expert Advisors. Add it exactly, then remove the EA from the chart and put it back.
Enter your license key in the EA inputsThe key field is empty. Open the EA properties and paste the key from your purchase email.
Invalid license keyUsually a copy error: a missing character, or a space picked up from the email. Copy it again.
This license key belongs to a different productYou have pasted the key of another Wisfli product. Each one has its own.
Account limit reachedThe key is already in use on three accounts. Free one at api.wisfli.com/manage.
License expiredThe subscription ended. Existing positions are still managed; no new ones are opened until you renew.
Cannot verify the license (no connection)A short outage is tolerated. If it persists, the terminal or the VPS is blocking outbound HTTPS.
Free backtest versionYou attached the backtest build to a live chart. Use it in the Strategy Tester, or install the file from your purchase email.
Trading messages
The panel shows stacks but nothing ever tradesNormal for long stretches. Two conditions of a stack have to hold on the same closed bar, and one position at a time means the EA sits out while a trade is open.
BLOCKED(SPREAD_nn)The spread was above the ceiling when the signal came. Raise the ceiling only if your broker's normal spread on gold is genuinely wider.
BLOCKED(DAILY_LOSS_LIMIT)The daily loss stop has fired. Entries resume the next day.
BLOCKED(MAX_TRADES_DAY) or (COOLDOWN)Working as intended: five entries a day, five bars between them.
risk is not enough for the minimum lotYour balance, at 0.5% risk and a 600-point stop, cannot fund 0.01 lots. Either add funds, raise the risk knowingly, or switch to fixed lots and accept the larger risk.
sell failed, ret=…The broker rejected the order. The retcode text says why — usually trading disabled, market closed, or not enough margin.
